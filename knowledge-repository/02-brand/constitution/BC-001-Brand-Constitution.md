------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part I --- Constitutional Standing

## 1. Purpose

The Brand Constitution defines how the Republic's constitutional
identity shall be expressed to the world.

It does not prescribe visual identity, campaigns or marketing assets.
Those are derived expressions.

Its purpose is to ensure that every future expression of the Republic
remains faithful to its constitutional identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Constitutional Authority

This document derives its authority from FCDP-001.

Where a future brand expression conflicts with this Constitution, this
Constitution prevails unless formally amended through constitutional
governance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part II --- The Purpose of the Brand

The Republic's brand exists to make its constitutional identity visible.

Its role is not merely to attract attention, but to faithfully
communicate who the Republic is, what it believes and why it exists.

Every expression should strengthen recognition, trust and belonging.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part III --- The Identity the Brand Expresses

The brand shall consistently express that the Republic is:

-   A place of hospitality.
-   A place of human connection.
-   A place where relationships are strengthened.
-   A place where belonging is cultivated.
-   An enduring institution rather than a single restaurant.

The brand should always reveal the Republic before it promotes products.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part IV --- The Brand Promise

The brand shall consistently reinforce the Republic's constitutional
promise:

-   Every person will be treated with dignity.
-   Hospitality will be generous.
-   Relationships matter more than transactions.
-   Trust will be earned.
-   The Republic exists to create opportunities for human connection.

Every public expression should strengthen these commitments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part V --- Brand Character

The Republic's character is enduring.

Its expressions should be:

-   Warm
-   Welcoming
-   Authentic
-   Generous
-   Respectful
-   Confident without arrogance
-   Refined without exclusivity
-   Hopeful
-   Timeless

Character guides expression more deeply than style.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part VI --- Brand Voice

The Republic's voice should be:

-   Human before corporate.
-   Clear before clever.
-   Warm before formal.
-   Confident before boastful.
-   Gracious before persuasive.

The voice should always reflect genuine hospitality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part VII --- Brand Integrity

The Republic's brand shall never intentionally become:

-   Manipulative.
-   Arrogant.
-   Transactional.
-   Superficial.
-   Deceptive.
-   Disrespectful.
-   Exclusive in spirit.
-   Inconsistent with constitutional purpose.

Integrity protects trust across generations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part VIII --- Constitutional Derivation

This Constitution authorises future brand artefacts including:

-   Brand Standards
-   Visual Identity Standards
-   Logo Standards
-   Colour Standards
-   Typography Standards
-   Photography Standards
-   Packaging Standards
-   Signage Standards
-   Digital Brand Standards
-   Advertising Standards
-   Social Media Standards

These documents implement this Constitution but do not redefine it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part IX --- Closing Declaration

The Republic's brand is not its logo.

It is not its colours.

It is not its advertising.

The Republic's brand is the visible expression of its constitutional
identity.

Every future expression should enable people to recognise not merely the
Republic's appearance, but its character, purpose and promise.
