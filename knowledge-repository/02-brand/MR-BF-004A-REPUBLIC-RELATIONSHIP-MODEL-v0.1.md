# Purpose

This document defines the four constitutional relationship communities of Meat Republik.

The Republic is not defined by transactions. It is defined by relationships.

> **The Republic grows through relationships.**

Every meaningful relationship with the Republic belongs to one of four communities.

---

# Constitutional Philosophy

> Food creates the moment.  
> The moment creates the memory.  
> The memory strengthens the relationship.  
> **The relationship builds the Republic.**

---

# 1. Citizens

**Those who belong to the Republic.**

Citizens are the heart of the Republic.

Illustrative progression:

- Visitor
- Resident
- Citizen
- Senior Citizen
- Statesman

Citizenship recognises belonging, not spending.

---

# 2. Custodians

**Those entrusted to care for and deliver the Republic.**

The Republic does not simply employ people.

It entrusts Custodians with preserving the Republic through every interaction.

Their responsibility extends beyond service to protecting:

- hospitality
- traditions
- quality
- memories
- relationships
- the Republic itself

Operational offices, ministries and stewardships will be defined under Operational Governance.

---

# 3. Friends of the Republic

**Those who strengthen the Republic through friendship, partnership and shared purpose.**

Friends of the Republic may include:

- producers
- suppliers
- artists
- musicians
- venue partners
- community organisations
- universities
- charities
- strategic collaborators

Friendship with the Republic is a recognition of shared purpose rather than citizenship.

---

# 4. Institutions

**The enduring structures that preserve, govern and evolve the Republic.**

Illustrative constitutional institutions include:

- Founder
- Citizen Council
- Senate
- Constitutional Offices
- Future Republic Committees

Institutions preserve continuity across generations.

---

# Relationship Test

Every initiative should strengthen at least one constitutional relationship community while never weakening another.

---

# Constitutional Statement

> The Republic belongs to its Citizens, is cared for by its Custodians, strengthened by its Friends, and preserved through its Institutions.
