# MR-BF-001 — BRAND CONSTITUTION (DOCUMENT BLUEPRINT)

**Subtitle:** Constitutional Blueprint for the Brand Constitution

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Founder Working Blueprint

---

# Purpose

This blueprint defines the intent, scope and internal structure of the Meat Republik Brand Constitution before the full constitutional document is written.

The objective is to ensure the Constitution answers the right questions, avoids overlap with neighbouring documents and becomes a stable constitutional reference.

# Why this Document Exists

The Brand Constitution establishes the permanent constitutional rules that define the identity of Meat Republik.

It should answer:

- What is the Republic?
- What must always remain true?
- What may evolve?
- What should never be compromised?

---

# What this Document Must NOT Contain

The Constitution should not contain:

- Marketing campaigns
- Operating procedures
- Menu specifications
- Technology choices
- Organisation charts
- Implementation detail

Those belong in later documents.

# Key Questions to Resolve During Authoring

1. What makes Meat Republik a Republic rather than simply a restaurant brand?
2. Which beliefs are constitutional and therefore difficult to change?
3. What principles belong in the Constitution versus the Republic Doctrine?
4. What defines citizenship?
5. What constitutional promises are made to citizens?

---

# Success Criteria

The Constitution is successful when:

- It can guide decisions for decades.
- Future documents derive naturally from it.
- It remains concise, timeless and implementation-independent.
- It protects the identity of the Republic as it evolves.

---

# Founder Note

The Constitution should be read as the highest governing brand document after the Republic Manifesto. It defines enduring truths rather than operational practices.
