# Preamble

We establish this Constitution to preserve the enduring identity of Meat Republik.

Its authority is derived from the Republic Manifesto, which remains the Constitutional Source Document for the Republic's founding beliefs.

The Constitution does not replace the Manifesto; it adopts, protects and governs its enduring truths.

# Part II — Constitutional Anchors

## CA-001 — Foundational Causal Chain

**Source:** MR-BF-000 — Republic Manifesto

> Food creates the moment.  
> The moment creates the memory.  
> The memory strengthens the relationship.  
> The relationship builds the Republic.

**Constitutional Role**

This Constitutional Anchor defines the Republic's foundational belief about how lasting relationships are intentionally created. All Constitutional Articles shall be interpreted consistently with this anchor.

---

# Part III — Constitutional Articles

## Article I — Identity

The Republic shall always be understood in accordance with Constitutional Anchor CA-001.

Meat Republik is a relationship-centred food brand organised as a symbolic Republic.

## Article II — Purpose

The Republic exists to strengthen relationships through shared food experiences.

## Article III — Citizenship

Citizenship is founded on participation, trust and belonging.

The detailed constitutional interpretation of citizenship shall be defined in MR-BF-004.

## Article IV — Relationships

Relationships are the Republic's primary purpose and measure of long-term success.

## Article V — Hospitality

Hospitality shall intentionally create meaningful moments capable of becoming lasting memories.

## Article VI — Trust

Trust shall be earned through consistent conduct, honesty and quality.

## Article VII — Stewardship

Growth shall strengthen the Republic without weakening its identity.

## Article VIII — Constitutional Evolution

This Constitution may be amended only through Founder-governed constitutional change while remaining faithful to the Constitutional Anchors adopted from the Manifesto.
