# Purpose

The Republic Doctrine provides the authoritative interpretation of the Brand Constitution.

The Constitution establishes what governs.

The Doctrine explains how those constitutional articles should be understood, interpreted and applied consistently across the Republic.

The Doctrine does not create new constitutional authority. It interprets existing constitutional authority.

# Responsibilities

The Doctrine shall:

- interpret each Constitutional Article;
- resolve ambiguity where multiple interpretations are possible;
- explain constitutional intent;
- establish interpretive principles;
- identify implications for future documents.

The Doctrine shall not:

- amend the Constitution;
- introduce new constitutional beliefs;
- contradict Constitutional Anchors.

# Success Criteria

The Doctrine is successful when:

- every Constitutional Article has one authoritative interpretation;
- downstream authors can apply the Constitution consistently;
- ambiguity is reduced without changing constitutional meaning;
- all interpretations remain traceable to the Constitution and, where applicable, the Manifesto.
