# Purpose

This document defines the complete architecture of the Brand Foundation domain before the remaining Brand Foundation documents are authored.

Its purpose is to ensure every document has a clear responsibility, authority, dependency and place within the repository.

# Dependency Principles

1. Higher-authority documents shall exist before lower-authority documents.
2. Every document has one primary purpose.
3. Every document declares its governing sources.
4. Cross-domain documents shall reference Brand Foundation rather than duplicate it.

---

# Exit Criteria

Brand Foundation is complete when:

- Constitutional documents are approved.
- Core frameworks are approved.
- Canonical terminology is established.
- Brand identity can govern all downstream domains without ambiguity.
