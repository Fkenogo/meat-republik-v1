# Preamble

The Republic Doctrine provides the authoritative interpretation of the Brand Constitution.

It does not create constitutional authority. It explains the constitutional intent of the Articles and Constitutional Anchors so they can be applied consistently throughout Meat Republik.

---

# Part I — Principles of Constitutional Interpretation

The Doctrine shall always interpret the Constitution according to these principles:

1. The Republic Manifesto remains the Constitutional Source Document.
2. Constitutional Anchors shall be interpreted as written, not paraphrased.
3. The Constitution governs; the Doctrine interprets.
4. Interpretations must preserve long-term identity over short-term convenience.
5. When uncertainty exists, the interpretation that best preserves the Republic's purpose shall prevail.

---

# Part II — Interpretation of Constitutional Anchor CA-001

## Anchor

> Food creates the moment.
> The moment creates the memory.
> The memory strengthens the relationship.
> The relationship builds the Republic.

## Interpretation

This is the Republic's causal model.

It establishes that food is not the end goal of Meat Republik. Food initiates a sequence that culminates in stronger human relationships and a stronger Republic.

Therefore, decisions should not optimise only for food quality or commercial performance in isolation. They should also consider whether they create meaningful moments, memorable experiences and stronger relationships.

---

# Part III — Interpretation of the Constitutional Articles

## Article I — Identity

The Republic is defined by the relationships it helps create, not merely by the meals it serves.

## Article II — Purpose

Every initiative should contribute to strengthening relationships through shared food experiences.

## Article III — Citizenship

Citizenship is a sense of belonging earned through participation and trust, not simply customer status.

## Article IV — Relationships

Relationships are the primary constitutional outcome against which success should ultimately be judged.

## Article V — Hospitality

Hospitality is the deliberate creation of welcoming moments that support the Constitutional Anchor.

## Article VI — Trust

Trust accumulates over repeated experiences and should not be compromised for short-term gain.

## Article VII — Stewardship

Growth is acceptable only when it reinforces the Republic's identity and constitutional purpose.

## Article VIII — Constitutional Evolution

Future constitutional amendments must preserve the intent of the Constitutional Anchors even when the wording of lower-order documents evolves.

---

# Part IV — Constitutional Conflict Resolution

When two interpretations appear possible:

1. Follow the Constitutional Anchor.
2. Follow the relevant Constitutional Article.
3. Prefer the interpretation that best strengthens relationships.
4. Escalate unresolved constitutional ambiguity to Founder governance.
