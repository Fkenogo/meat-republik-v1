# 1. Purpose

This document defines what it means to belong to Meat Republik.

Citizenship is not a loyalty programme, a marketing tier or a rewards mechanism.
It is the constitutional expression of a relationship with the Republic.

# 2. Constitutional Doctrine

The Republic exists to strengthen relationships.

> Food creates the moment.  
> The moment creates the memory.  
> The memory strengthens the relationship.  
> **The relationship builds the Republic.**

Every citizenship decision shall therefore ask:

**"Will this strengthen the relationship between the Republic and its people?"**

# 3. Principles of Citizenship

- Belonging is earned through relationship, not purchased.
- Citizenship recognises commitment rather than spend.
- Recognition follows contribution as well as participation.
- Every citizen is equal in dignity.
- Offices and honours are separate from citizenship status.

# 4. Citizenship Journey (Working Model)

## Visitor
Experiences the Republic for the first time.

## Resident
Returns voluntarily and has begun building an ongoing relationship with the Republic.
A Resident may or may not yet hold a formal Republic identity.

## Citizen
Has intentionally joined the Republic and is recognised as belonging to it.

## Senior Citizen
A Citizen recognised for sustained participation, positive influence and long-term commitment.

## Statesman
A Citizen recognised for preserving, strengthening and advancing the Republic over time.

# 5. Civic Honours

These are recognitions, not progression levels.

- Honorary Citizen
- Founder

Additional honours may be introduced through constitutional governance.

# 6. Civic Offices

These are responsibilities, not citizenship ranks.

Illustrative future offices include:

- Ambassador
- Citizen Councillor
- Senator

Appointments shall be governed independently from citizenship status.

# 7. Constitutional Test

A citizenship model is successful when it produces stronger relationships, deeper belonging and greater stewardship of the Republic rather than merely increasing transactions.

# Founder Note

The Republic is built one relationship at a time.

Citizenship is therefore recognition that a relationship has become part of the Republic itself.
