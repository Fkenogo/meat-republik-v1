# BF-001 --- Brand Architecture Framework

**Document ID:** BF-001\
**Title:** Brand Architecture Framework\
**Status:** Draft --- Founder Design\
**Authority Level:** Constitutional Framework (B1)\
**Parent Authority:** BC-001 --- Brand Constitution\
**Derived From:** FCDP-001 --- Constitutional Blueprint of the Republic

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part I --- Purpose

The Brand Architecture Framework defines how the Republic's brand is
organised, extended and expressed across present and future initiatives.

Its purpose is to ensure that growth strengthens the Republic's identity
rather than fragments it.

This framework governs relationships between brands. It does not define
visual identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part II --- Architectural Principles

The Republic adopts the following principles:

-   One constitutional identity.
-   One master brand.
-   Many faithful expressions.
-   Growth through coherence.
-   Every expression strengthens the Republic.

No new brand should weaken recognition of the Republic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part III --- Master Brand

The Republic is the constitutional master brand.

Every future expression derives legitimacy from the Republic and should
reinforce, rather than compete with, its identity.

The master brand communicates purpose, trust and continuity across
generations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part IV --- Expressions of the Republic

Authorised expressions may include:

-   Republic Houses
-   Signature dining experiences
-   Products and menu collections
-   Community initiatives
-   Educational programmes
-   Digital Republic experiences
-   Future ventures authorised through governance

Expressions are encouraged to develop distinct operational identities
while remaining recognisably part of the Republic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part V --- Naming Architecture

Names should:

-   Reflect the Republic's constitutional character.
-   Be simple, memorable and enduring.
-   Strengthen recognition of the Republic.
-   Avoid unnecessary complexity or fragmentation.
-   Support future expansion.

Naming decisions should favour coherence over novelty.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part VI --- Endorsement Model

Every derived brand should clearly communicate its relationship to the
Republic.

Possible endorsement patterns include:

-   "A Republic House"
-   "By the Republic"
-   "Part of the Republic"

The precise visual implementation is defined in future brand standards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part VII --- Brand Portfolio Governance

Before a new brand, programme or experience is established, it should
answer:

-   Does it advance the Republic's purpose?
-   Does it strengthen the master brand?
-   Does it belong within an existing family?
-   Is a new expression genuinely required?
-   Will it remain understandable twenty years from now?

Architectural simplicity is preferred over unnecessary proliferation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part VIII --- Future Derivations

This framework authorises development of:

-   Brand Standards
-   Visual Identity Framework
-   Naming Standard
-   Logo Standard
-   Brand Portfolio Register
-   Endorsement Guidelines
-   Brand Extension Policy

These documents implement this framework and shall remain consistent
with BC-001.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part IX --- Closing Declaration

The Republic's brand architecture exists to preserve unity while
enabling growth.

As the Republic expands into new Houses, experiences, communities and
ventures, every expression should make the Republic more recognisable,
more trusted and more meaningful.

Growth is successful when people recognise the Republic not only by its
name, but by its enduring character.
