# Preamble

The Brand Principles translate the Constitution and the Republic Doctrine into practical decision guidance.

They do not create new constitutional authority. They guide how choices should be made when designing, operating and evolving the Republic.

---

# Principle 1 — Create Moments Before Meals

**Derived From**

- Constitutional Anchor CA-001
- Article II — Purpose

**Guidance**

Every product, service or interaction should be designed to create a meaningful moment, not merely complete a transaction.

**Decision Test**

Will this create a moment worth remembering?

---

# Principle 2 — Design for Memories

**Derived From**

- Constitutional Anchor CA-001

**Guidance**

Experiences should be memorable because memorable experiences strengthen relationships.

**Decision Test**

What memory are we intentionally creating?

---

# Principle 3 — Strengthen Relationships

**Derived From**

- Article IV — Relationships

**Guidance**

When choosing between alternatives, prefer the one that is more likely to strengthen long-term relationships.

**Decision Test**

Will people feel closer after this experience?

---

# Principle 4 — Grow Belonging

**Derived From**

- Article III — Citizenship

**Guidance**

Every citizen should increasingly feel that Meat Republik is a place where they belong.

**Decision Test**

Does this increase belonging?

---

# Principle 5 — Practise Genuine Hospitality

**Derived From**

- Article V — Hospitality

**Guidance**

Hospitality is measured by how valued people feel rather than by the efficiency of service alone.

**Decision Test**

Will citizens leave feeling more valued?

---

# Principle 6 — Protect Trust

**Derived From**

- Article VI — Trust

**Guidance**

Never sacrifice long-term trust for short-term advantage.

**Decision Test**

Would we still be proud of this decision in five years?

---

# Principle 7 — Preserve the Republic While It Evolves

**Derived From**

- Article VII — Stewardship

**Guidance**

Innovation is encouraged only when it strengthens the Republic's identity.

**Decision Test**

Does this strengthen the Republic without changing who we are?

---

# Principle Application

These principles apply to every downstream framework, including:

- Citizen Experience
- Menu & Product Development
- Operations
- Brand Expression
- Digital Experiences
- Partnerships
- Community Programmes
