# 1. Purpose

MR-EA-002 defines the capabilities Meat Republik must possess in order to exist, operate, grow and remain constitutionally aligned.

It describes **what the Republic must be able to do**.

It does not prescribe the detailed organisation, technology, process or staffing used to deliver those capabilities. Those decisions belong to downstream frameworks, standards, operating models and implementation programmes.

The Capability Architecture creates a stable bridge between:

- constitutional intent;
- enterprise domains;
- operating models;
- programmes;
- repository documents;
- implementation work.

---

# 2. Capability Architecture Principles

The Republic Capability Architecture is governed by the following principles:

1. Capabilities describe enduring abilities, not temporary projects.
2. Capabilities are expressed as outcomes the Republic must be able to produce.
3. A capability may be delivered by people, processes, partners, physical environments or digital systems.
4. Capabilities shall remain distinct from organisational departments.
5. Every capability must have one accountable governing domain.
6. Every capability must trace upward to enterprise and constitutional intent.
7. Downstream documents may specialise a capability but shall not redefine its enterprise purpose.
8. The architecture shall remain simple enough to guide action and detailed enough to prevent structural gaps.
9. Capabilities may mature over time without changing their core identity.
10. The architecture must serve the Republic rather than create administrative burden.

The Republic Capability Architecture contains ten Level 1 capability families:

1. Govern the Republic
2. Define and Protect the Brand
3. Understand and Serve Citizens
4. Create Food and Shared Experiences
5. Operate the Republic
6. Express the Republic
7. Enable the Digital Republic
8. Sustain the Commercial Model

10. Learn, Improve and Evolve

# 7. Capability Dependencies

The following high-level dependencies apply:

```text
Constitutional Governance
        ↓
Brand Definition
        ↓
Citizen Understanding
        ↓
Offering and Experience Design
        ↓
Operations and Brand Expression
        ↓
Digital, Commercial and Community Enablement
        ↓
Measurement, Learning and Evolution
```

These dependencies are directional rather than strictly sequential.

Several capabilities will develop in parallel, but lower-order design should not proceed without sufficient clarity in the capabilities that govern it.

# 8. Capability Maturity Model

Each capability may be assessed using the following maturity states:

| Maturity State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| M0 — Unrecognised | The capability has not yet been formally identified. |
| M1 — Defined | Purpose, scope and ownership are documented. |
| M2 — Designed | Frameworks, requirements and operating design exist. |
| M3 — Implemented | The capability is operating in practice. |
| M4 — Controlled | Performance, controls and responsibilities are established. |
| M5 — Improving | Evidence is used to continuously improve the capability. |

Capability maturity does not itself grant approval to proceed. Readiness and authority remain governed through the Master Development Programme and relevant domain governance.

---

# 9. Capability Governance Requirements

Every Level 2 capability shall eventually declare:

- capability owner;
- governing domain;
- purpose;
- scope;
- exclusions;
- constitutional and enterprise dependencies;
- required documents;
- operating model dependencies;
- measures;
- risks;
- maturity state;
- improvement priorities.

These declarations may be maintained in later capability registers or domain frameworks rather than repeated inside this enterprise architecture.

---

# 10. Relationship to Repository Documents

The Capability Architecture does not require one document for every capability.

A single framework may govern several related capabilities, while a complex capability may require multiple standards, policies and procedures.

Every downstream repository document should identify the capability or capabilities it governs, enables or implements.

Recommended metadata extension:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Governed Capability | CAP-CX-04 — Deliver Hospitality |
| Enabled Capabilities | CAP-CX-05, CAP-CO-07 |
| Capability Maturity Contribution | M1 → M2 |

# 12. Exclusions

MR-EA-002 does not:

- define organisational departments;
- appoint capability owners;
- specify staffing structures;
- define technology architecture;
- prescribe detailed business processes;

- replace domain frameworks;
- create new constitutional beliefs;

# 14. Review and Evolution

The Capability Architecture should be reviewed when:

- a new enterprise domain is proposed;
- a material operating model change is proposed;
- a major product or experience category is introduced;
- new digital or commercial capabilities materially change the Republic;
- recurring work cannot be mapped to an existing capability;
- duplicate or conflicting capability ownership emerges;
- constitutional evolution changes enterprise intent.

Changes to this architecture shall follow governed change and shall not be made through informal repository restructuring.

---

# Founder Note

The Republic Capability Architecture exists to keep Meat Republik focused on what it must be able to do, rather than allowing documents, departments or systems to define the enterprise by accident.

Capabilities are the enduring abilities of the Republic.

Programmes develop them.

Documents govern and describe them.

People, processes, places, partners and technology bring them to life.
