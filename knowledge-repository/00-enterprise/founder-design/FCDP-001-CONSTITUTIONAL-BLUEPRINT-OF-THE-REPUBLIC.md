## 1. Purpose

This document records the foundational design of Meat Republik as a
constitutional enterprise and serves as the Founder source record from
which future constitutional instruments will be derived.

## 2. Constitutional Declaration

> The Republic was founded on the belief that food has the power to
> bring people together, hospitality has the power to make people feel
> they belong, and meaningful relationships are among life's greatest
> treasures.

> The Republic therefore exists to create opportunities for human
> connection through remarkable hospitality, exceptional food and shared
> experiences that strengthen relationships and cultivate belonging.

> Every interaction is an opportunity to strengthen a relationship.

> Everyone who enters the Republic should leave feeling more connected
> than when they arrived.

> **The meal may end at the table, but the Republic should continue in
> people's lives.**

## 3. Founding Proposition

Meat Republik is not merely a restaurant business.

It is a constitutional enterprise designed to create the conditions in
which people connect, relationships strengthen and belonging grows.

Food creates the moment.

The moment creates the memory.

The memory strengthens the relationship.

The relationship creates belonging.

Belonging builds the Republic.

## 4. Constitutional Purpose

The Republic exists to create opportunities for human connection through
remarkable hospitality, exceptional food and shared experiences that
strengthen relationships and cultivate belonging.

## 5. Enduring Ambition

The Republic aspires to become one of the places that matters in
people's lives.

People should feel able to say:

-   I want to be there.
-   I miss being there.
-   I can't wait to meet you there.
-   I want to take you there.

## 6. Constitutional Success

Operational success sustains the enterprise.

Constitutional success fulfils the Republic's purpose by creating
opportunities for connection, strengthening relationships, cultivating
belonging and continuing to matter in people's lives.
