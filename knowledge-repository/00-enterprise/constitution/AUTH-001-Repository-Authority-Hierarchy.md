# 1. Purpose

This document defines how authority is distributed throughout the
Republic's Knowledge Repository.

Its purpose is to ensure that every document has an appropriate level of
authority, an identifiable approval path and a clear relationship to the
constitutional foundations of the Republic.

Authority is intended to protect constitutional integrity while allowing
operational knowledge to evolve responsibly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Authority Principles

The Republic adopts the following authority principles:

-   Authority flows from constitutional foundations to operational
    implementation.
-   Lower-level documents shall not contradict higher-level documents.
-   Interpretation is permitted; constitutional redefinition is not.
-   Every governed document declares its authority level.
-   Every amendment follows the approval requirements of its authority
    level.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Amendment Authority

Different authority levels require different governance.

-   **A1--A2:** Founder approval following constitutional review.
-   **B1:** Founder approval after architectural review.
-   **B2:** Delegated governance approval within constitutional
    boundaries.
-   **C1--D2:** Managed through operational governance provided they
    remain consistent with higher authority.

No lower-level document may amend or supersede a higher-level document.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Conflict Resolution

Where two documents appear to conflict:

1.  The document with the higher authority prevails.
2.  If authority is equal, the newer approved version prevails unless
    explicitly stated otherwise.
3.  Constitutional ambiguity shall be referred for Founder determination
    before implementation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Required Metadata

Every governed document should include:

-   Document ID
-   Title
-   Status
-   Authority Level
-   Parent Authority
-   Derived From
-   Approval Authority
-   Version
-   Effective Date
-   Supersedes / Superseded By (where applicable)

This metadata enables governance, traceability and orderly evolution.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Stewardship Responsibilities

Every Custodian responsible for a governed document shall:

-   preserve constitutional fidelity;
-   maintain traceability;
-   avoid unnecessary duplication;
-   retire obsolete knowledge through governance;
-   ensure successors can understand the document's purpose and
    authority.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Closing Statement

The Repository Authority Hierarchy provides the constitutional order of
the Republic's knowledge ecosystem.

By making authority explicit, the Republic ensures that growth does not
compromise coherence, that innovation does not undermine identity and
that future generations inherit a knowledge system that is both
trustworthy and understandable.
