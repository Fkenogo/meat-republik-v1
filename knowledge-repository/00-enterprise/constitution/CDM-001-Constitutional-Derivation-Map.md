------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Purpose

The Constitutional Derivation Map defines how constitutional authority
flows from the Republic's founding principles into every framework,
standard, guide and operational artefact.

Its purpose is to ensure that every governed document has a clear
constitutional lineage and that no constitutional document exists
without an authorised place within the Republic's knowledge
architecture.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Derivation Principles

The Republic adopts the following principles:

-   Every governed document has a single primary constitutional parent.
-   Authority flows downward, never upward.
-   Derived documents may reference multiple artefacts but inherit
    authority from one primary parent.
-   New constitutional principles originate only through amendment of
    constitutional instruments.
-   Operational documents implement constitutional intent; they do not
    redefine it.

# 6. Document Derivation Pattern

Each governed document should declare:

-   Parent Authority
-   Derived From
-   Constitutional Lineage
-   Knowledge Domain
-   Authority Level

Example:

``` text
HS-002 Hospitality Standard

Parent:
CX-001 Republic Experience Framework

Derived From:
FCDP-001 §23.3
"Hospitality is an act of generosity."

Knowledge Domain:
Experience
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Repository Growth Rules

The Republic's repository shall evolve by extending existing
constitutional families wherever practical.

Creation of a new constitutional family requires Founder approval and
constitutional governance.

This approach minimises duplication and preserves architectural
coherence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Architectural Traceability

Every constitutional statement should be traceable to:

-   the framework that interprets it;
-   the standard that operationalises it;
-   the guidance that explains it;
-   the procedure that executes it.

Conversely, every implementation artefact should be traceable back to
its constitutional source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Closing Statement

The Constitutional Derivation Map provides the architectural blueprint
through which the Republic's knowledge ecosystem grows.

Its purpose is not to restrict innovation but to ensure that every
future document strengthens the Republic's constitutional integrity,
preserves institutional memory and remains faithful to its enduring
purpose.
