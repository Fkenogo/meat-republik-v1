**Status:** Draft --- Founder Design\
**Authority Level:** Constitutional Instrument\
**Parent Authority:** FCDP-001 --- Constitutional Blueprint of the

**Programme:** MR-KA-001 --- Constitutional Knowledge Architecture

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article I --- Purpose

The Knowledge Repository exists to preserve, organise, govern and
transmit the constitutional memory of the Republic.

It is more than a document store. It is the institutional memory through
which the Republic preserves its identity, philosophy, governance and
accumulated wisdom across generations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article II --- Constitutional Standing

The Knowledge Repository is recognised as a constitutional institution
of the Republic.

Its purpose is to ensure that knowledge remains authentic, traceable,
governed and accessible to future Custodians.

No document derives constitutional authority merely by existing within
the repository. Authority is established through constitutional
governance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article III --- Constitutional Principles

The Republic adopts the following principles for all constitutional
knowledge:

1.  Knowledge serves the Republic before individuals.
2.  Knowledge shall preserve constitutional intent.
3.  Knowledge shall be governed rather than merely stored.
4.  Knowledge shall be traceable to its constitutional source.
5.  Knowledge shall evolve through authorised governance.
6.  Knowledge shall preserve institutional memory.
7.  Knowledge shall remain understandable by future generations.
8.  Knowledge shall distinguish enduring principles from operational
    practice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article IV --- Knowledge Domains

The Repository is organised into enduring knowledge domains rather than
organisational departments.

Illustrative domains include:

-   Enterprise
-   Brand
-   Experience
-   People
-   Republic Houses
-   Commercial
-   Governance
-   Knowledge
-   Innovation

Additional domains may be established through constitutional governance
where required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article V --- Knowledge Categories

Every document shall belong to a recognised category.

The principal categories are:

-   Constitution
-   Constitutional Framework
-   Standard
-   Guide
-   Procedure
-   Template
-   Operational Record
-   Reference

Each category carries a defined purpose and authority level.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article VI --- Authority and Lineage

Every governed document should declare, where applicable:

-   Document Identifier
-   Authority Level
-   Parent Authority
-   Derived From
-   Approval Authority
-   Status
-   Effective Version
-   Supersedes
-   Superseded By

Authority flows downward from constitutional instruments to
implementation artefacts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article VII --- Knowledge Lifecycle

Every governed document follows a managed lifecycle:

Design

↓

Review

↓

Approval

↓

Publication

↓

Revision

↓

Supersession

↓

Archive

The Repository preserves historical versions to maintain institutional
memory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article VIII --- Constitutional Traceability

Every constitutional principle should be capable of being traced into
the framework, standard or procedure that implements it.

Likewise, every operational document should be capable of tracing its
authority back to the constitutional principle from which it derives.

Bidirectional traceability is a defining characteristic of the
Republic's knowledge system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article IX --- Stewardship

Knowledge belongs to the Republic.

Founders establish it.

Custodians steward it.

Institutions preserve it.

Future generations inherit it.

Every Custodian who contributes knowledge acts as a steward rather than
an owner.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Repository Governance Model

The Repository is governed as a constitutional ecosystem.

``` text
Founder Intent
        ↓
Constitution
        ↓
Constitutional Frameworks
        ↓
Standards
        ↓
Guides
        ↓
Procedures
        ↓
Templates
        ↓
Operational Records
```

Each layer derives its authority from the layer above it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Constitutional Interpretation

This Constitution governs the Republic's knowledge architecture.

It does not replace operational governance, nor does it define business
processes. Its purpose is to establish the enduring rules by which
knowledge is created, classified, governed, preserved and inherited.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Closing Declaration

The Republic recognises that enduring institutions are sustained not
only by faithful people, but also by faithfully preserved knowledge.

This Constitution therefore establishes the Knowledge Repository as the
guardian of the Republic's constitutional memory, ensuring that future
generations inherit not merely documents, but the purpose, reasoning and
principles that gave rise to them.
