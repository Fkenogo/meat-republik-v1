# Purpose

This document is the authoritative catalogue of all enterprise domains used by Meat Republik.

Each domain defines a coherent body of responsibility. Programmes, frameworks, standards and procedures are organised beneath exactly one domain.

# Ownership Rules

1. Every repository document belongs to one primary domain.
2. Cross-domain relationships are expressed through references, not duplicated content.
3. Domain frameworks govern their own standards, policies and procedures.
4. Constitutional content originates only in the Brand Foundation constitutional layer.
